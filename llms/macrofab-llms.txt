# MacroFab

MacroFab is a US-based electronics manufacturing services (EMS) company with a digital manufacturing platform. We help hardware engineering teams quote, order, and track PCB assemblies — from prototype to production — through an online platform powered by FabIQ, MacroFab's manufacturing AI.

## What MacroFab Does

MacroFab provides contract electronics manufacturing with a technology-first approach:
- PCB assembly (PCBA) for prototypes and production runs
- Full supply chain management and component sourcing
- Component inventory management: consign parts to a MacroFab warehouse and allocate across builds
- Order tracking with real-time production visibility
- ITAR-registered manufacturing for defense and aerospace programs
- Box build, fulfillment, and inventory management

## The MacroFab Platform

MacroFab's manufacturing platform is powered by FabIQ, an AI system that automates quoting, sourcing, and supply chain intelligence. Engineers upload design files (Gerbers, ODB++, native EDA formats) and receive instant, itemized quotes. The platform includes:
- Live BOM pricing against distributor inventory
- Component alternate suggestions with form-fit-function comparison
- Tariff impact analysis at the component level
- Consigned inventory management with BoM-level coverage and real-time consumption tracking
- Order tracking from design review through shipment
- Revision history and side-by-side order comparison
- Volume pricing curves from prototype through production
